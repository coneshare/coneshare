## What's New in V1.2.0

- Activity-based workflow automation from key events such as document views, downloads, and dataroom access.
- Slack and webhook integrations to route engagement signals to internal workflows in real time.
- Delivery logs with retry and replay support for production reliability and operational visibility.
- Multi-destination delivery so one event can notify several systems and teams at once.

![Automation rule configuration screenshot](/screenshots/blog/v1-2-0-automation-rules.png)

## Why This Matters

V1.2.0 strengthens Coneshare as a secure document sharing and dataroom platform with actionable activity automation. Teams can detect engagement earlier, coordinate faster, and follow up when interest is highest.


## Expanded Use Cases

### 1. Investor Awareness and Timing

Founders can trigger instant internal alerts when investors open or revisit a pitch deck. This helps teams follow up while the conversation is still warm, instead of waiting for manual updates.


### 2. Sales Follow-Up Automation

Sales teams can send proposal activity events to Slack or webhook endpoints, then trigger follow-up tasks when a document is viewed or downloaded. This reduces response time and improves team coordination.


### 3. Dataroom Deal Visibility

Deal teams can monitor dataroom engagement and route activity signals into internal systems. This gives better pre-call context on what buyers reviewed and where interest is increasing.

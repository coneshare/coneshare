## Datarooms Now Support Brand and Reading Flow Control

When sensitive documents are shared externally, presentation and review flow matter.

The first impression, navigation clarity, and reading sequence all shape how confidently recipients engage with a dataroom.

Coneshare V1.3 introduces branded datarooms and structured item ordering to help teams create more intentional external review experiences.

Coneshare works as a control and intelligence layer on top of existing storage systems like Nextcloud, Google Drive, and Dropbox.

V1.3 continues that direction by improving how shared datarooms are presented and consumed during fundraising, diligence, legal review, and enterprise collaboration workflows.

## What’s New in V1.3

- Dataroom branding settings (banner + theme colors).
- Manual mixed-item ordering for folders and documents.

## 1) Present Datarooms with More Trust and Context

Dataroom owners can now configure branding directly on each room:

- branding banner;
- primary brand color;
- secondary brand color;
- accent color.

![Branded dataroom setting](/screenshots/blog/v1-3-0-branded-dataroom-setting.png)


In high-stakes workflows, branding is not just visual polish.

It helps recipients immediately recognize organizational context and makes shared datarooms feel more intentional and trustworthy.

![Branded dataroom view](/screenshots/blog/v1-3-0-branded-dataroom.png)


## 2) Guide Reviewers Through the Right Reading Flow

V1.3 adds owner-controlled ordering for mixed dataroom items.

Teams can now define the exact sequence of folders and documents shown inside a dataroom.

This helps investors, buyers, legal teams, and clients review information in the intended order, reducing context switching and making complex reviews easier to navigate.

![Dataroom ordering UI](/screenshots/blog/v1-3-0-ordering-ui.png)


## Why This Release Matters

V1.3 strengthens the external dataroom experience in two practical ways:

- **Presentation quality:** datarooms can now reflect team branding and feel less generic.
- **Review efficiency:** controlled ordering helps viewers consume content in the intended sequence.

Combined with existing Coneshare controls (passwords, expiration, verification, watermarking, download settings), this release makes dataroom sharing more ready for real-world decision workflows.

Teams can now create more polished review experiences without changing where documents are stored.

## Also Included in V1.3

V1.3 also includes share link UX and backend reliability fixes. For full technical details, see the [release changelog](https://docs.coneshare.com/en/release-notes/).

V1.3.0 is available now for [self-hosted deployments](https://docs.coneshare.com/en/).

## Next Steps

- Upgrade to V1.3.0 and apply branding on your key datarooms.
- Reorder top-level and folder-level items to match your external review narrative.

Every document or dataroom in Coneshare can have **multiple independent share links**, letting you apply stricter or looser rules per recipient group without duplicating files.

## Link Security Controls

When creating or editing a share link, configure only the protections that fit your workflow:

- **Password protection:** Require visitors to enter a shared passphrase before previewing.
- **Email verification:** Require recipients to confirm their email address (or restrict access by domain/allowlist) so every view is tied to a verified identity.
- **Expiration date:** Automatically revoke access after a deal closes or a review window ends.
- **Download permissions:** Keep files view-only in the browser or permit downloads when external teams need local copies.
- **NDA / agreement gating:** Require recipients to accept confidentiality terms before viewing materials.

## Updating Files Without Breaking Links

1. Open the document details view and upload a new version.
2. Existing share links continue working automatically with the same URL and security rules.
3. New visitor sessions open the latest version immediately without forcing you to resend links.

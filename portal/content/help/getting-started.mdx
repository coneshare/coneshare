Coneshare adds a controlled, trackable sharing and dataroom layer around your team&apos;s documents—without forcing you to rebuild your storage habits.

## Core Concepts

| Space | Best For | Key Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Library** | Staging files and sharing single documents | Nested folders, single-file share links, version history |
| **Virtual Datarooms** | Multi-document deals, due diligence, client portals | Custom branding, per-link folder permissions, Q&amp;A, ZIP download |
| **File Requests** | Collecting files from external parties | Dedicated upload links with expiration, limits, and traceability |

## Recommended First Workflow

1. **Upload or import a file:** Add PDFs, slide decks, spreadsheets, or videos directly, or connect [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive), [Dropbox](/integrations/dropbox), or [Nextcloud](/integrations/nextcloud).
2. **Choose your delivery surface:** Create a single-document share link for quick outreach, or group multiple materials into a [Virtual Dataroom](/help/datarooms).
3. **Set recipient rules:** Add password protection, email verification, expiration dates, download toggles, or [Dynamic Watermarks](/help/dynamic-watermarking) per link.
4. **Review activity:** Check visitor sessions, page-by-page dwell times, and real-time notifications as stakeholders read.

> Looking for self-hosting Docker installation or `app.env` settings? Visit the [Server Admin Docs](https://docs.coneshare.com/en/quick-start/).

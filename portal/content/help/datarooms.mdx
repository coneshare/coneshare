A Virtual Dataroom (VDR) groups documents and nested folders into a single external portal—ideal for investor due diligence, client onboarding, RFPs, and legal reviews.

## Setting Up a Dataroom

1. **Create the dataroom:** Name your room and add folders and documents from your library or cloud integrations.
2. **Customize branding:** Upload a room logo, choose a single banner or image carousel, and optionally enable file index numbers for structured diligence checklists.
3. **Create audience-specific links:** Generate separate links (for example, *Initial Investors* vs. *Legal Counsel*) and adjust folder visibility, download permissions, and watermarks per link.
4. **Enable collaboration:** Invite internal teammates to manage folders together, turn on **Q&amp;A** for structured buyer questions, or allow one-click **ZIP downloads** for authorized visitors.

## Dataroom vs. Personal Folders

- Use **Personal Folders + single links** when sharing one file at a time.
- Use a **Dataroom** when recipients need structured multi-folder navigation or different permission tiers across the same materials.

# Coneshare: Features & Solutions

Coneshare is an enterprise-grade, self-hosted document sharing and virtual data room platform. It provides the advanced security, control, and analytics of leading services like DocSend, but in a package that you control on your own infrastructure.

---

# Features

## Secure Document & Dataroom Sharing

Share sensitive documents with confidence. Coneshare's link-based sharing system gives you complete control over who sees your content and how they access it.

### Key Capabilities

- **Link-Based Sharing:** Share single documents or entire data rooms using secure links. Our in-browser viewer supports all major file types without requiring recipients to download software or create an account.
- **Granular Access Control:** Protect every link with robust security settings:
  - **Password Protection:** Secure links with an encrypted password.
  - **Email Verification:** Require viewers to verify their email address before gaining access.
  - **Download Restrictions:** Allow or prohibit downloading on a per-link or even per-file basis within a data room.
  - **Link Expiration:** Set links to automatically expire by a specific date and time.
- **Live Document Updates:** Fix a typo or update a file by uploading a new version. All existing share links will automatically point to the latest version, ensuring everyone stays on the same page.

## Advanced Analytics & Tracking

Gain actionable insights into how your shared documents are being consumed. Coneshare provides real-time analytics to help you prioritize engagement and optimize your content.

### Key Capabilities

- **Real-Time View Notifications:** Opt-in to receive an email notification the moment someone views your shared link.
- **Page-by-Page Analytics:** Understand what content resonates most with detailed, page-by-page analytics, including time spent on each page and overall completion rate.
- **Visitor & Session Insights:** See who is viewing your documents, where they are from (GeoIP), what device they are using, and whether they downloaded the file.
- **Centralized Dashboard:** A main dashboard provides a high-level overview of recent activity, including the latest view sessions and most recently active links.

## Dynamic Watermarking

Prevent unauthorized sharing and protect intellectual property with dynamic watermarks that are applied on the fly.

### Key Capabilities

- **Dynamic Content:** Automatically embed viewer-specific information into the watermark, such as their **email address** or **IP address**.
- **Customizable Text:** Define custom watermark text to suit your needs, such as "Confidential" or "Draft".
- **Pervasive Protection:** Watermarks are tiled across the entire document and applied to both in-browser previews and downloaded PDF files, ensuring persistent protection.

## Virtual Data Rooms (VDRs)

Organize, manage, and share collections of documents and folders in a secure, structured environment.

### Key Capabilities

- **Easy Setup and Management:** Create and organize unlimited data rooms in minutes. Add existing documents, create nested folders, and manage content with an intuitive interface.
- **Granular Link Permissions:** When sharing a data room, you retain fine-grained control. Set unique visibility, download, and watermarking rules for every individual file and folder within that specific share link.
- **Folder Downloads:** Allow viewers to download entire folders as a single ZIP archive. The system intelligently respects all permissions, only including the files the viewer is permitted to download and applying watermarks where required.

## Cloud Storage Integration

Bridge the gap between public and private clouds. Seamlessly bring files from your existing cloud storage into Coneshare, whether it's on a major public service or your own self-hosted server.

### Key Capabilities
- **Connect Your Accounts:** Securely connect to popular public cloud services like **Dropbox** and **Google Drive**, as well as self-hosted private cloud platforms like **Nextcloud**, all via standard OAuth2.
- **In-App File Browser:** Browse your connected cloud drives from within the Coneshare UI.
- **Asynchronous Imports:** Select files to import, and Coneshare's background workers will handle the download and processing, ensuring the UI remains responsive.

## Self-Hosted & Enterprise-Ready

Built from the ground up for security-conscious organizations, Coneshare runs entirely on your own infrastructure.

### Key Capabilities

- **Total Data Sovereignty:** With no reliance on third-party services, you have complete control over your data, ensuring compliance with regulations like GDPR and HIPAA.
- **Admin Control Panel:** A dedicated admin panel allows superusers to manage dynamic application settings (like file size quotas) and user accounts.
- **User Quotas:** Set and enforce per-user storage quotas to manage resource consumption.
- **Containerized Deployment:** Deploy the entire stack with ease using the provided Docker Compose configuration.

# Solutions

## Coneshare for Secure Fundraising (Venture Capital & Startups)

Streamline your fundraising process from pitch to due diligence with a platform you control.

- **Control Your Pitch Deck:** Share your deck using a secure link and track investor engagement page by page. See which slides are getting the most attention and identify truly interested parties. If an investor passes, deactivate their link instantly.
- **Secure Data Rooms for Due Diligence:** Create VDRs for serious investors. Use granular permissions to reveal documents in stages, require email verification, and apply dynamic watermarks with the investor's email address to deter leaks.
- **Live Updates:** Made a change to your financial model? Upload a new version, and all existing data room links are instantly updated. No more "Final_v2_final" confusion.

> "As a VC, we see hundreds of pitch decks. The ones sent via a secure, self-hosted platform like Coneshare immediately stand out. It shows the founders are serious about security and gives us confidence. The ability to track our engagement also means their follow-ups are always relevant."

## Coneshare for Regulated Industries (Legal, Finance, Healthcare)

Share sensitive client information and case files with the assurance of complete data sovereignty and auditability.

- **Meet Compliance Requirements:** By self-hosting Coneshare, you ensure all data stays within your network, helping you meet strict compliance standards like HIPAA or GDPR without trusting a third-party vendor with your sensitive files.
- **Client Portals:** Use Datarooms to create secure client portals. Share discovery documents, contracts, and reports. Granular permissions ensure clients only see the files relevant to them.
- **Protect Intellectual Property:** When sharing M&A documents or clinical trial results, enforce download restrictions and apply dynamic watermarks with the recipient's IP address and viewing time.

> "Our law firm can't afford to have client data on a multi-tenant cloud service. Coneshare gives us the features of a modern VDR with the security of an on-premise solution. It's an essential part of our toolchain for M&A deals."

## Coneshare for Sales & Marketing Teams

Arm your sales team with the insights they need to close deals faster and ensure brand consistency.

- **Track Proposal Engagement:** Stop guessing if your proposal was read. Get notified when a prospect opens your document and see exactly which pages they focused on. Tailor your follow-up call to address their specific interests.
- **Centralized Content Library:** Manage all your sales collateral—brochures, case studies, price sheets—in one place. When you update a document, every link your sales team has ever sent is automatically updated to the latest version.
- **Secure Prospect Data Rooms:** Create a dedicated Dataroom for high-value prospects. Share demo videos, technical specifications, and contracts in one organized space, all branded with your company logo.

> "Before Coneshare, we were just sending PDF attachments into the void. Now, our sales team can focus their energy on prospects who are actually engaged with our materials. Our follow-up conversations are more strategic, and our deal velocity has noticeably improved."
